# Data Export

The **Data Export** tab allows you to easily query, filter, and download customized subsets of your spatial metadata and gene expression as a CSV file for further analysis.

*   Spatial Lasso Filter: To export a specific tissue region, draw a lasso in the Interactive Explorer, save the cell set, and upload the resulting CSV here.
*   Apply rules (e.g., 'Cluster == Macrophage') to isolate specific cell populations.
*   Choose which metadata properties to include in your final download.
*   Search for individual genes or paste a bulk list to append their raw expression counts as new columns in your output CSV.
